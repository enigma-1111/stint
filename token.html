Price and volume come from the public pool feed. Holder count comes from the explorer. The fee line is 1% of that volume, not a claim receipt. If nobody trades, it is zero.

Where a swap fee goes

Every swap on the pool pays 1%. That fee is split in kind, on both sides of the pool.

  1. 90% to holders, in both $STINT and WETH, in proportion to what a wallet holds.
  2. 5% to the Bankr treasury.
  3. 5% to a BNKR buyback.

The holder share streams over 24 hours on the pool. Anyone can poke a distribute. You claim it on pools.fun, in a new tab, not on this page. The pool, the locker, and the burn address do not earn. This is not a payout for writing, and not a promise of income.
