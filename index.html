How the penny works

  1. Read the story. Write the next passage.
  2. Every character costs one cent, including spaces and punctuation.
  3. Connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain and pay that many USDG.
  4. When the payment lands, the story reloads for everyone on this page.

Several people can pay at once. The chain orders the payments. Minimum 20 characters. Maximum 800 per turn. Gas is ETH.
